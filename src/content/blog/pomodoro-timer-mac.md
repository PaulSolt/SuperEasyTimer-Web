Most Pomodoro apps want you to build a system: projects, tags, session logs, streaks. Super Easy Timer takes the other path. Type "pomodoro" and start.

## Switch modes by typing

There is no settings maze. Type "pomodoro" in the timer field and the app switches to Pomodoro mode. Work in focused blocks, take your breaks, and get back to it.

## Keep the countdown where you can see it

A Pomodoro only works if you notice it. Pin the timer [always on top](/blog/always-on-top-timer-mac) of your editor, keep it in the [menu bar](/blog/menu-bar-timer-mac), or shrink it into a corner. The countdown stays in view while you work, even over full screen apps.

## Repeat without restarting

Focus sessions come in rounds. Turn on [repeat with an optional delay](/blog/repeat-timer-with-delay) and the next block starts on its own. Add a flash or an alarm at zero so the end of a session is impossible to miss.

## No system to maintain

No accounts. No charts to feed. No streak to protect. When you are done, close the timer and your work is what you have to show for it.

Study apps keep inventing new things to manage: streaks, forests, leaderboards, virtual study rooms. The actual requirement is smaller. Decide how long to study, see the time while you work, and notice when it ends.

## Type the session and start

"45 min" for a reading block. "25" for a Pomodoro-length sprint. "2h" for a long review session with breaks. Type it, press Return, study.

## Small enough to ignore, visible enough to work

Shrink the timer into a corner with [Mini Mode](/blog/resize-timer-mini-mode), pin it [above your notes and browser](/blog/always-on-top-timer-mac), or keep it in the [menu bar](/blog/menu-bar-timer-mac). It sits in your peripheral vision, which is exactly where a study timer belongs.

## Breaks that start themselves

Turn on [repeat with a delay](/blog/repeat-timer-with-delay): study block, break, study block. The next session starts without negotiation, which is the point. For classic Pomodoro sessions, type "pomodoro" and use [Pomodoro mode](/blog/pomodoro-timer-mac).

## See where the time went

Turn on [count up after zero](/blog/count-up-after-zero) to see how far past the block you kept going, or type "stopwatch" to measure an open-ended review session.

No account, no streak to lose, nothing to feed. The timer does its job so you can do yours.

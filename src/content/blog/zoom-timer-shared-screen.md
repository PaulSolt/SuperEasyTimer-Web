On a video call, time disappears. The clock is behind the meeting window, the agenda is in another tab, and the demo that was supposed to take ten minutes is on minute twenty-five. Put a timer on the call.

## Keep the timer visible while you present

Super Easy Timer stays [pinned on top of other apps](/blog/always-on-top-timer-mac), including full screen meeting windows. Start a countdown before you share your screen and the timer stays in your view while you talk: your own private pace clock.

## Or share it with the whole call

Want everyone to see the time? Place the timer inside the window or screen you are sharing, and the countdown becomes part of the meeting. Breakout sessions, workshops, and timed exercises run themselves when every participant watches the same number.

## Time-box the segments

Type "10 min" for the demo, "5" for questions, "3pm" to count down to the hard stop. Adjust on the fly with "Add 2 min" or "+2m" while the timer runs. More in the [meeting timer guide](/blog/meeting-timer).

## Keep it readable, not distracting

Shrink the timer into a corner with [Mini Mode](/blog/resize-timer-mini-mode), or pick a [transparent theme](/blog/timer-themes-transparent-frosted-liquid-glass) so it sits quietly over your slides. It is there when you glance, invisible when you focus.

> Try the [free web timer](/timer) in a browser tab you share. The Mac app is what stays visible over the full screen meeting window.

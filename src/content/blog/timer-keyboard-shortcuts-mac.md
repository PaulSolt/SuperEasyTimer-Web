Switching apps to pause a timer costs more than the pause. You leave your editor, find the timer window, click, and now you are wondering what you were doing. Global keyboard shortcuts remove the trip.

## Pause, resume, and reset from anywhere

Super Easy Timer's global shortcuts work from any app. Deep in your editor, mid-email, presenting: press the shortcut and the timer pauses, resumes, or resets. You never leave what you are doing.

## Made for hands that stay on the keyboard

The whole app works this way. [Type "25 min"](/blog/type-to-start-timer) to start, "Add 5 min" or "+5m" to adjust [without restarting](/blog/add-time-without-restarting), and shortcuts for the controls. The mouse is optional.

## Presentation-safe controls

Shortcuts matter most when clicking is not an option. While your slides are up, the timer stays [pinned above the presentation](/blog/presentation-timer-for-keynote) and responds to shortcuts without touching the slideshow. Pause for the unexpected question, resume when you are back on script.

## Pairs with every placement

Menu bar, [floating](/blog/floating-timer-mac), mini, full screen: the shortcuts work the same wherever the timer lives. Keep it small in the corner and run it entirely from the keyboard.

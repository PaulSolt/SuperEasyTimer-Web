Tabata is simple on paper: work hard, rest, repeat. Keeping count while you are out of breath is the hard part. Super Easy Timer counts the rounds so you do not have to.

## Type "tabata" to start

Type "tabata" in the timer field and the app switches to Tabata mode. Work intervals, rest intervals, and rounds run as a cycle. Your job is the workout, not the clock.

## Hear it and see it

Mid-burpee is no time to squint at a screen. Turn on the flash so the whole timer signals the change, and pick an alarm sound that cuts through. Make the timer large enough to read from across the room, or take it [full screen](/blog/full-screen-countdown-timer-mac).

## Rest between rounds, automatically

The timer moves from work to rest and back on its own. No taps between intervals, no reaching for the keyboard with chalk on your hands.

## One timer for the whole session

Warm up with a plain countdown, run your Tabata rounds, then type a stretch timer at the end. The same app covers the whole workout.

Set it once, press start, and train.

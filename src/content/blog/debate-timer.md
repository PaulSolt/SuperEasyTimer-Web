Debate formats are strict: constructives, rebuttals, cross-examination, prep time, each with its own clock. The timing should be the least contested thing in the room.

## Type the speech length

"8 min" for a constructive. "3 min" for cross-ex. "4" for a rebuttal. Type the time, press Return, and the speech clock is running. Switching between speech lengths is a line of typing, not a settings screen.

## Everyone sees the same clock

Show the countdown [full screen](/blog/full-screen-countdown-timer-mac) or large on a shared display so both teams and the judges watch the same number. No disputes about whose phone was right.

## A clear, neutral signal at zero

The timer can flash and sound an alarm when time expires. The signal comes from the screen, not from a judge's call, and it is the same for both sides.

> Try the [free web timer](/timer) for a single speech. The Mac app adds the flash, count up, and full screen for the room.

## Track prep time by counting down

Each team's prep bank is just another countdown: type "5 min" when they start using it, pause when they stop. Pause and resume without losing the clock.

## Overtime on the record

With [count up after zero](/blog/count-up-after-zero), a speaker who talks past the signal accumulates visible overtime. The number is on the screen for the whole room.

Sometimes the whole room needs to see the time. Super Easy Timer turns your Mac into a full screen countdown with big, clear digits.

## Set it up in three steps

1. Type the time: "25 min", "90s", or just "10". Press Return.
2. Choose the full screen placement mode. The countdown fills the display.
3. On a projector or TV, mirror your display, or move the timer to that display first.

The digits scale to fit, so they read clearly from the back of the room. Nothing else gets in the way: no menu bar, no desktop, no notifications.

> Try the [free web timer](/timer) full screen in your browser right now. The Mac app adds themes, add-time, and the end-of-timer options.

## Built for shared screens

Use the full screen countdown for classes, talks, meetings, webinars, and any shared display. Set a time, start it, and let everyone watch the same clock. Need more time mid-session? Type "+5m" and the countdown [adjusts without a restart](/blog/add-time-without-restarting).

## Pick the look that fits the room

Choose a [theme](/blog/timer-themes-transparent-frosted-liquid-glass) that suits the space. A bright screen for a classroom, or a calm dark one for a talk. The timer keeps your theme in full screen.

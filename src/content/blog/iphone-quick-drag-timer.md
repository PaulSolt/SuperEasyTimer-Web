For the times you use every day, skip the keyboard. Press the + button in Super Easy Timer, slide your finger up to the time you want, and let go.

## Drag to a time

1. On the Timer tab, press and hold the **+** button.
2. Slide up. The picker steps through your quick times as you go.
3. Let go on the one you want.

With the default settings, letting go sets the timer and starts it. Two switches in the Settings tab, in the **Quick Actions** section and the **Timer Input** section, change that.

## The two switches

| Setting | Default | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Start Quick Actions Instantly** | On | Letting go picks the time | The picker stays open; tap ✓ to confirm |
| **Auto-Start Quick Actions** | On | The picked time starts counting | The picked time waits for Play |

Turn off **Start Quick Actions Instantly** if you tend to let go one step early and want a chance to check. Turn off **Auto-Start Quick Actions** if you like to pick the time while you set up and tap Play when you start.

**Start timers automatically** is a different switch. It covers typed times only, so changing it won't change what the drag does.

## When the picker stays open

Sometimes the picker waits for you even with **Start Quick Actions Instantly** on:

- **You dragged to the very top.** The picker parks there so you can pick calmly instead of on release. Tap the time you want, then ✓.
- **You use VoiceOver or Switch Control.** The picker always uses the confirm step, so each choice is announced before it's set.

Either way, finish the visible step, then look for the seconds moving. A picked time and a running timer look alike for the first second.

If you went through the app's tutorial and missed the target a few times in a row, the tutorial turns **Start Quick Actions Instantly** off for you. Turn it back on in Settings whenever you like.

## Choose the times in the picker

In the Settings tab, under **Quick Actions**, tap **Customize** to choose which times appear when you drag. Put your three most-used times in, and the drag gets shorter.

**Sound Effects** in the same section turns the drag's sounds on or off. **Haptics** next to it covers the app's vibrations in general, including the ticks you feel as you slide.

## Drag or type?

Drag for the times you repeat: a 5-minute break, a 25-minute focus block. Type when you need an exact time like `7 min` or `1h 15m`; the [typed-input guide](/blog/type-to-start-timer/) lists what you can type, with Mac and iPhone steps.

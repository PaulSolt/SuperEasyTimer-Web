<p class="capture-note">Captured in the iPhone simulator with Super Easy Timer 3.0.1; the App Store version may look different. The picker image shows confirmation mode, with Start Quick Actions Instantly off.</p>

For the times you use every day, skip the keyboard. Press the + button in Super Easy Timer, slide your finger up to the time you want, and let go.

## Drag to a time

1. On the Timer tab, press and hold the **+** button.
2. Slide up. The picker steps through your quick times as you go.
3. Let go on the one you want.

With the default settings, letting go sets the timer and starts it. Two switches in the Settings tab, in the **Quick Actions** section and the **Timer Input** section, change that.

## The two switches

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="Timer settings comparison" tabindex="0">

| Setting | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start Quick Actions Instantly** | Letting go picks the time | The picker stays open; tap ✓ to confirm |
| **Auto-Start Quick Actions** | The picked time starts counting | The picked time waits for Play |


</div>

Both switches are on by default. Turn off **Start Quick Actions Instantly** if you tend to let go one step early and want a chance to check. Turn off **Auto-Start Quick Actions** if you like to pick the time while you set up and tap Play when you start.

**Start timers automatically** is a different switch. It covers typed times only, so changing it will not change what the drag does.

Before you look away, check that the seconds are moving. A picked time and a running timer look alike for the first second.

## Choose the times in the picker

In the Settings tab, under **Quick Actions**, tap **Customize**, then **Timer**, to choose which times appear when you drag. Put the times you use most in the list.

## Drag or type

Drag for the times you repeat: a 5-minute break, a 25-minute focus block. Type when you need an exact time like `7 min` or `1h 15m`; the [typed-input guide](/blog/type-to-start-timer/) lists what you can type, with Mac and iPhone steps.

Set up your quick times once, and your everyday timers start with one drag.

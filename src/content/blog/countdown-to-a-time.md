Half the countdowns in a day are not durations. They are deadlines. The call at 2:30. The oven at 6. The end of the workday. Instead of computing "how many minutes until then," type the time itself.

## Type the clock time

Type "5pm" and press Return. The timer calculates the gap and counts down to five o'clock. "2:15pm", "10:30am", "9pm": any clock time works. The am or pm tells the timer you mean a time of day, not a duration.

## No mental math

It is 1:47 and the meeting is at 3:15. That is... some number of minutes. You do not care, and you do not have to. The timer does the subtraction and shows the answer counting down.

## Watch the deadline approach

Keep the countdown [pinned above your work](/blog/always-on-top-timer-mac) or in the [menu bar](/blog/menu-bar-timer-mac), and the time-until-deadline is always one glance away. Counting down to a hard stop in a meeting? Put it [on the shared screen](/blog/meeting-timer) so everyone sees the runway.

## Durations still work

The same field takes both forms. "45 min" when you care about the length, "5pm" when you care about the moment. [Type what you mean](/blog/type-to-start-timer) and the timer figures out which one it is.

Deadlines are clock times. Now your countdown speaks that language too.

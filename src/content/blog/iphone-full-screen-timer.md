Prop your iPhone against a mug, a book or a stand, and it becomes a desk clock for your task. Fullscreen gives the countdown the whole screen, so you can read it from your chair while you rehearse, write on paper, or work on something that is not the phone.

## Open Fullscreen

1. Type the time, such as `25`, and tap the blue up arrow. If the display holds at 25:00, tap Play; with **Start timers automatically** on, it is already running.
2. Once the timer starts, tap the **Fullscreen** button, the two-arrow icon at the top-right of the timer card.
3. Set the phone down where you will look at it.

Tap anywhere on the countdown to show or hide its controls. You can pause there and tap the triangle button to resume, without leaving Fullscreen.

To leave, tap to show the controls, then tap X.

## Portrait or landscape

Either works. Landscape usually makes the digits bigger when the phone lies on its side against something.

To have turning the phone open Fullscreen for you, turn on **Rotate to Go Full Screen** in the Settings tab. It is off by default. Turning the phone back upright does not close Fullscreen; tap X for that. Closing keeps the orientation the phone is in.

## Stop the screen from going dark

A countdown you are watching from across the desk is no use if the phone locks when its Auto-Lock delay runs out. In the Settings tab, turn on **Prevent Auto-Lock While Timing**.

It keeps the screen on while a timer is running and Super Easy Timer is open, and it is off by default. If you pause, switch apps or lock the phone yourself, normal Auto-Lock comes back. A lit screen uses battery, so plug in for a long session.

## Make it easy to read from where you sit

Check it from your working position, not while it is in your hand. A countdown that is clear at arm's length can be hard to read beside a notebook with a lamp reflecting off it. Angle the phone away from the light.

Start the timer, tap Fullscreen, and set the phone where you can glance at it while you work.

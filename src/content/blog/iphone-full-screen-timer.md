Prop your iPhone against a mug, a book or a stand, and it becomes a desk clock for your task. Fullscreen gives the countdown the whole screen, so you can read it from your chair while you rehearse, write on paper, or work on something that isn't the phone.

## Open Fullscreen

1. Type the time, such as `10 min`, and submit it. If the display holds at 10:00, tap Play; with **Start timers automatically** on, it's already running.
2. Tap the **Fullscreen** button, the two-arrow icon at the top-right of the timer card.
3. Set the phone down where you'll look at it.

Tap anywhere on the countdown to show or hide its controls. Pause and Play are there, so you don't have to leave Fullscreen to stop for a minute.

To leave, tap to show the controls, then tap X.

## Portrait or landscape

Either works. Landscape usually makes the digits bigger when the phone lies on its side against something.

To have turning the phone open Fullscreen for you, turn on **Rotate to Go Full Screen** in the Settings tab. It's off by default. Turning the phone back upright doesn't close Fullscreen; tap X for that. Closing keeps the orientation the phone is in.

## Stop the screen from going dark

A countdown you're watching from across the desk is no use if the phone locks when its Auto-Lock delay runs out. In the Settings tab, turn on **Prevent Auto-Lock While Timing**.

It keeps the screen on while a timer is running and Super Easy Timer is open, and it's off by default. If you pause, switch apps or lock the phone yourself, normal Auto-Lock comes back. A lit screen uses battery, so plug in for a long session.

## Make it easy to read from where you sit

Check it from your working position, not while it's in your hand. A countdown that's clear at arm's length can be hard to read beside a notebook with a lamp reflecting off it. Angle the phone away from the light.

## The ending

Fullscreen is about seeing the time. The sound at the end is set separately: tap the **Timer settings** button (the slider icon) on the timer, open **Alarms & Sound**, and pick a sound other than **No sound**. With the app open, that sound plays at the phone's volume.

If you might lock the phone or leave the app before it ends, also turn on **Use System Alarm** and allow alarms when iOS asks; if you declined before, tap **Allow System Alarms**. Lock the phone through a one-minute timer once to hear the ending you'll get.

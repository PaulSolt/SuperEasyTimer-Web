Some timers are for you. This one is for the room. When a class, a workshop, or a meeting needs to share one clock, the digits have to be big.

## Drag it as large as you need

Super Easy Timer resizes from a [tiny corner timer](/blog/resize-timer-mini-mode) to a display-filling countdown. Drag it to the size the room needs. The digits stay crisp and readable at any size.

## Or take the whole screen

For maximum visibility, go [full screen](/blog/full-screen-countdown-timer-mac): one number, edge to edge, on the projector or the TV. The back row reads it as easily as the front.

## High contrast for hard rooms

Bright rooms, cheap projectors, glare: pick a [theme](/blog/timer-themes-transparent-frosted-liquid-glass) with the contrast the room needs. Black background with light digits for a dark room, white or cream for a bright one.

## Start it without slowing the room down

Type "10 min" and press Return, and the big countdown is running. Change it mid-session with "Add 5 min" or "+5m" while everyone watches the number update: no restart, [no lost time](/blog/add-time-without-restarting).

Workshops, classrooms, gyms, kitchens, retros: when the whole room shares one clock, the room keeps itself on time.

An interval timer is just a countdown that starts itself again. Super Easy Timer does exactly that: set a time, turn on repeat, and the timer loops until you stop it.

## Set the interval, turn on repeat

Type the interval: "20 min", "45s", "5". Turn on [repeat](/blog/repeat-timer-with-delay) and the countdown restarts automatically at zero. Add an optional delay between rounds when the loop needs a gap: rest between sets, a pause between rotations.

## Intervals are everywhere

- Posture and eye breaks: a 20 minute loop that reminds you to look away from the screen.
- Practice rotations: "10 min" per station, flashing at each change.
- Kitchen check-ins: stir every 5 minutes without watching the pot.
- Work sprints: repeated focus blocks, or type "pomodoro" for [Pomodoro mode](/blog/pomodoro-timer-mac).
- Workouts: rounds and rest, or type "tabata" for [Tabata mode](/blog/tabata-timer-mac).

## Signals you can trust

Each round can end with a flash and an alarm that repeats until you notice. The interval does its job even when you are not looking at the screen.

## Keep the loop in view

Pin the interval timer [above your windows](/blog/always-on-top-timer-mac) or keep it [small in a corner](/blog/resize-timer-mini-mode). Glance up, see where the round stands, keep going.

No presets to program, no laps to configure. A countdown on a loop covers more than you would think.

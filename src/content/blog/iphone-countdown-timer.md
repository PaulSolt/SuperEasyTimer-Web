When you know how long you need, type it. In Super Easy Timer, type `25` and you have a 25-minute countdown.

## Set your first countdown

1. Tap the time input.
2. Type `25`.
3. Tap the blue up arrow to submit.
4. If the display holds at 25:00, tap Play. If the seconds are already moving, **Start timers automatically** is on and it is running.

## What you can type

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="Timer input examples" tabindex="0">

| Type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| `5 min` or `5m` | Five minutes |
| `25` | 25 minutes; a plain number above zero means minutes (`0` starts a stopwatch) |
| `1h 30m` | An hour and a half |
| `90s` | 90 seconds |
| `5pm` | A countdown to 5 p.m. |


</div>

While a timer runs, `+5m` adds five minutes and `-2m` takes two off, without starting over. More entries, with Mac steps too, are in the [typed-input guide](/blog/type-to-start-timer/).

## Choose whether submitting starts the timer

The Settings tab has two start switches. **Start timers automatically** covers typed durations. **Auto-Start Quick Actions** covers times picked by dragging up from the + button.

<div class="table-scroll" role="region" aria-label="Timer settings comparison" tabindex="0">

| Setting | Off | On |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start timers automatically** | Submit, then tap Play | Submitting starts the countdown |
| **Auto-Start Quick Actions** | Pick a time, then tap Play | The picked time starts |


</div>

By default, typed auto-start is off and quick-action auto-start is on: a typed duration waits for Play and a dragged one starts on its own. A clock time like `5pm` starts right away either way. Leave typed auto-start off when you want to set up first: type `10 min`, get your notes in order, and tap Play when you begin. Turn it on when you are always ready the moment you submit.

The drag-up gesture also has a confirm switch, **Start Quick Actions Instantly**, in the Settings tab's **Quick Actions** section.

## Pause, and pick it back up

Tap Pause if someone interrupts; tap the triangle button to resume from the same time. To start over at the full time, type it again and submit.

Type the time, tap the arrow, and keep the countdown in view.

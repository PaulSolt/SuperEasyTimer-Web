When you know how long you need, typing it is faster than spinning hour and minute wheels. In Super Easy Timer, type `5 min` and you have a five-minute countdown.

## Set your first countdown

1. Tap the time input.
2. Type `5 min`, and read the time the app shows it will set before you submit.
3. Submit the entry.
4. If the display holds at 5:00, tap Play. If the seconds are already moving, **Start timers automatically** is on and it is running.

Watch the seconds tick before you put the phone down. A time waiting on screen and a running countdown look alike at first glance.

## What you can type

| Type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| `5 min` or `5m` | Five minutes |
| `25` | 25 minutes; a plain number above zero means minutes (`0` starts a stopwatch) |
| `1h 30m` | An hour and a half |
| `90s` | 90 seconds |
| `5pm` | A countdown to 5 p.m. |

While a timer runs, `+5m` adds five minutes and `-2m` takes two off, without starting over. More entries, with Mac steps too, are in the [typed-input guide](/blog/type-to-start-timer/).

## Choose whether submitting starts the timer

The Settings tab has two start switches, and they cover different ways of setting a timer:

| Setting | Covers | Default | Off | On |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Start timers automatically** | Typed durations | Off | Submitting stages the time; you tap Play | Submitting starts the countdown |
| **Auto-Start Quick Actions** | Times picked by dragging up from the + button | On | The picked time waits for Play | The picked time starts |

So by default a typed duration waits for Play and a dragged one starts on its own. A clock time like `5pm` starts right away either way. Leave typed auto-start off when you want to set up first: type `10 min`, get your notes in order, and tap Play when you begin. Turn it on when you are always ready the moment you submit.

The drag-up gesture also has a confirm switch, **Start Quick Actions Instantly**, in the Settings tab's **Quick Actions** section.

## Pause, and pick it back up

Tap Pause if someone interrupts; tap Play to carry on from the same time. To start over at the full time, type it again and submit.

## Watch it, or leave the app

- **Fullscreen**, the two-arrow button at the card's top-right, gives the digits the whole screen.
- **Float**, the Picture in Picture button at the card's top-left, keeps a small countdown over other apps.
- After you leave the app, the countdown also shows on the Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have it, in the Dynamic Island, as long as Live Activities are allowed for Super Easy Timer in the iOS Settings app.

To hear the ending with the phone locked, tap the **Timer settings** button (the slider icon) on the timer and open **Alarms & Sound**. Turn on **Use System Alarm**, allow alarms when iOS asks, and pick a sound other than **No sound**. If you declined permission before, tap **Allow System Alarms** on that screen. Then lock the phone through a one-minute timer to hear the ending you chose.

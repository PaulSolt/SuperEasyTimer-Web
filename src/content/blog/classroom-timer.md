"You have five minutes" works better when the five minutes is on the board. Super Easy Timer puts a large, readable countdown on the classroom display, and starting it takes one line of typing.

## Start a timer as fast as you say it

Type "5 min" and press Return. That is the whole setup. A bare number works too: "10" starts a 10 minute timer. When the projector is on and thirty students are waiting, that speed matters.

## Big enough for the back row

Take the timer [full screen](/blog/full-screen-countdown-timer-mac) so the countdown fills the display, or keep it large in a corner next to your slides. The digits are made to be read from a distance.

## Timers for transitions

Cleanup, station rotations, silent reading, lining up: classroom time is a chain of small countdowns. Turn on [repeat with a delay](/blog/repeat-timer-with-delay) for rotations, and use the flash at zero so the end of an activity is visible even when the room is loud.

## Count up when the class runs over

Turn on [count up after zero](/blog/count-up-after-zero) and the timer shows how far past the limit the discussion went. Useful for you, motivating for them.

No accounts, no ads on the board, no clutter. Just the time, large enough for everyone to own it.

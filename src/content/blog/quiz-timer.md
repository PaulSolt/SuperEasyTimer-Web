Quizzes move fast. Thirty seconds a question, next round, keep the energy up. The timer should keep pace without you touching it between questions.

## Repeat the same countdown for every question

Set the time per question: "30s", "1 min", "90s". Turn on [repeat](/blog/repeat-timer-with-delay) and the countdown restarts automatically after each question. Add a short delay between rounds for reading the next question aloud.

## Make the deadline visible

Show the timer large on the shared display, or take it [full screen](/blog/full-screen-countdown-timer-mac) between slides. The flash at zero gives the whole room the "pencils down" moment without you shouting over the table talk.

> Try the [free web timer](/timer) for a single round. The Mac app adds repeat, the flash at zero, and the pinned modes below.

## Change the pace mid-quiz

Lightning round? Type "15s" and press Return. Hard question? Type "Add 30s" while the timer runs. The countdown adjusts without a restart, and the quiz keeps its rhythm.

## Works next to your slides

Running the quiz from Keynote or PowerPoint? The timer can stay [pinned above the slideshow](/blog/presentation-timer-for-keynote), so the countdown and the question share the screen.
